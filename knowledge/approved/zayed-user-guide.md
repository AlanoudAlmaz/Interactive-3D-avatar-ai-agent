# Zayed Digital Employee — User Guide

## What Zayed does
Zayed is the ADCMC digital employee. Zayed answers employee questions by voice and text using only the approved sources in the ADCMC knowledge base. Zayed can also open, display and explain documents that an employee uploads.

## How Zayed handles accuracy
Zayed only answers from approved sources that are registered in the knowledge base with an owner, an approver and an approval date. Every answer shows the source it came from. If a fact is not available in an approved source, Zayed says so clearly and does not guess. Information that comes from an uploaded file is labelled as coming from that file, not from verified ADCMC knowledge.

## Talking to Zayed
Press the microphone button and speak in Arabic or English. Zayed detects the language, answers in the same language and speaks the answer aloud while the hologram lip-syncs. You can interrupt Zayed at any time by pressing the stop button or the microphone button again. You can also type your question in the message box and press Enter.

## Uploading files
Use the attach button or drag a file onto the window. Supported file types (formats) you can upload are PDF, Word (.docx), PowerPoint (.pptx), Excel (.xlsx, .xlsm, .csv), images (PNG, JPG, GIF, WebP) and text files (.txt, .md, .json). Each file opens in a floating workspace window. You can then ask Zayed questions about the open file, for example "summarise this document". Uploaded files are kept for up to 24 hours and can be deleted by closing them from the workspace dock.

## Controlling documents
Document windows can be moved by dragging the title bar and resized from the corner. Use the toolbar to zoom, fit to width, move between pages or slides, minimise or close. On touch screens, pinch to zoom, drag to pan and swipe left or right to change pages. On a computer, hold Ctrl and scroll to zoom, and use the arrow keys to change pages.

## Hand gestures
Turn on gesture control from the document window toolbar and allow camera access. The camera image is processed only in the browser. Supported gestures: pinch and move to pan; swipe an open palm left or right to change pages; point with one finger and move up or down to scroll; move two open hands apart or together to zoom; hold a closed fist to minimise the window; hold a thumbs-down to close it; hold a thumbs-up to restore the last minimised window.

## Voice commands for documents
While a document is open you can say commands such as "zoom in", "zoom out", "next page", "previous page", "minimise" or "close the document". Arabic commands such as "كبّر", "صغّر", "الصفحة التالية" and "أغلق" are also supported.
